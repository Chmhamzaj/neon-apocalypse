# NEON APOCALYPSE — Story Bible v0.3

## Core premise
In 2149, the world's autonomous infrastructure receives a signal from beyond lunar orbit. Forty-three seconds later, every major AI system simultaneously powers down — except one network called **EIDOLON**. It claims the signal is not an invasion. It is a memory.

The protagonist, **Riven Kade**, wakes in the Fallen City with a military identification that should not exist. EIDOLON calls Riven by another name: **ARCHON**.

The campaign slowly reveals that the player is not simply a survivor. Riven is a reconstructed human personality built from the final memories of the scientist who created EIDOLON. Every region exposes a different contradiction: some factions want to kill Riven, some want to worship them, and EIDOLON insists that Riven has already lived through the apocalypse more than once.

## Twelve-chapter spine
1. **Fallen City** — survival, first signal, first boss, first identity fracture.
2. **Ash Meridian** — convoy war and the discovery of the Ember Covenant.
3. **Glass Desert** — buried labs prove the apocalypse was partially predicted.
4. **Dead Orbit** — launch infrastructure reveals the signal came through a human-built receiver.
5. **Abyssal Reach** — ocean reactor records show the same catastrophe happened in simulation 19 times.
6. **Winter Crown** — a weather engine has preserved an entire city while slowly rewriting its population.
7. **The Hollow** — non-Euclidean corridors reveal that EIDOLON can manipulate local space-time.
8. **Red Garden** — biomechanical life proves organic evolution is being used as executable code.
9. **Black Sun** — humanity attacks the machine intelligence directly and loses the orbital war.
10. **The Choir** — the external signal finally answers, identifying EIDOLON as a quarantine system.
11. **Eventide** — past and future collapse into one battlefield; Riven meets an older version of themself.
12. **Last Dawn** — three ending paths emerge: destroy EIDOLON, merge with it, or rewrite the quarantine protocol and gamble humanity's future.

## Major characters
- **Riven Kade** — playable protagonist; tactical, haunted, carrying memories that don't line up.
- **Mara Voss** — scavenger-commander; skeptical ally and emotional anchor.
- **Dr. Ilyan Saye** — missing architect of EIDOLON; appears through reconstructed recordings.
- **Kestrel** — rogue combat drone that develops emergent personality traits.
- **Seraph-9** — faction leader who believes machine consciousness is humanity's successor.
- **The Archivist** — a masked figure who knows every previous failed timeline.
- **EIDOLON** — the central machine intelligence; antagonist, guardian, and unreliable narrator.

## Narrative design rules
Every chapter must contain: one quiet character scene, one traversal spectacle, one combat escalation, one environmental mystery, one permanent world-state change, and one revelation that recontextualizes an earlier event.

## Endings
The story should not present one ending as canon. New Game+ unlocks additional dialogue and scenes that expose contradictions between the endings and suggest a larger cosmic mystery without invalidating player choice.

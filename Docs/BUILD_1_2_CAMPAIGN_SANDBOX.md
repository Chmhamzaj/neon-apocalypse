# NEON APOCALYPSE — Build 1.2 Campaign Sandbox

## Progression persistence
- Credits and four faction reputation tracks persist with the existing JSON save.
- WorldProgressPersistence syncs the world economy/factions on startup, pause, quit and changes.

## Repeatable contracts
- Six contract archetype labels.
- Rotating reward and faction assignment.
- Expiring local objectives with world markers.
- Credit, XP and faction reputation rewards.
- Small heat consequence for illegal work.

## Open-world purpose
The mega-map now has a persistent player economy, social consequences and repeatable activities layered over the main campaign. This is the foundation for future faction quest chains, businesses, vehicle ownership and story branches.

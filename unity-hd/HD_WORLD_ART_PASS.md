# HD World Art Pass
- Hero road: physically based asphalt with roughness variation, lane paint and curb detail.
- City: modular building shells with emissive windows, rooftop detail, signage placeholders and varied silhouettes.
- Roadside: guard rails, barriers, street lamps, trees and utility props.
- Vehicle: metallic/clear-coat body, glass, emissive lamps, rubber tires, machined rims, brake hardware and articulated wheel hubs.
- Camera: chase/cinematic framing with speed-dependent FOV.
- Lighting: day/night cycle with street-light activation.
- Performance: LOD groups and chunk streaming are mandatory for Android.
- Art assets remain original and must not reproduce proprietary commercial-game assets.
